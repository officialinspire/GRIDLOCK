Grid Lock City

Local play 2–4 players · one device v0.8 papercraft

How To Play

The City

Grid Lock City is a 6 × 6 grid of empty city blocks laid out between intersections. No streets are paved yet.

Pave a Road

On your turn, tap the gap between two neighbouring intersections to pave one road. Players go in seat order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4.

Claim Blocks

Pave the last road around a block and it's yours: it gets your colour and flag. One road can close two blocks at once.

Keep Going

Claiming a block earns you another road straight away, so you can chain captures. If your road closes nothing, play passes on.

Money

Every mayor starts with $12,000. Each block you claim pays $500.

At the start of your turn you collect income from your developed blocks. Vacant lots earn nothing.

Develop

Claimed blocks start Vacant (Level 0). On your turn, tap one of your blocks to build Residential, Commercial, Park, Civic, Industrial or a Landmark, then upgrade it up to Level 3 for more income.

Costs are paid immediately. You can also leave a block vacant.

★ Bonuses: 3+ connected homes or shops form a district, parks boost the homes next to them, and a connected home + shop + park cluster earns a mixed-use bonus.

City Events

After every full round a city event is drawn: storms, fires, festivals, booms and recessions change income or building prices for a round or two.

Emergencies skip blocks inside a civic building's protection zone.

Debt & Bankruptcy

Every turn you pay upkeep: a small land tax plus maintenance on your buildings. If it pushes you below $0, sell or downgrade buildings (50% back) before you can play on.

If even that can't cover it, declare bankruptcy: your blocks are abandoned, the debt is wiped, and you restart with fresh capital. Other mayors can buy and restore abandoned blocks.

Winning

When every block is enclosed, the mayor with the highest City Value wins: cash + land + buildings.

Ties go to more blocks, then more developed blocks, then more cash. Awards like Most Blocks and Greenest City go to standouts too.
