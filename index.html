Grid Lock City

Local play 2–4 players · one device v0.1 foundation

How To Play

The City

Grid Lock City is a 6 × 6 grid of city blocks divided by streets. Downtown blocks in the middle are worth more than the suburbs on the edge.

Taking Turns

2–4 players share one device. On your turn, make your moves, then press End Turn to pass play to the next mayor.

Blocks & Buildings

Buy open blocks to grow your territory, then build homes, shops, parks and civic landmarks on them.

Buying & building arrive in the next phase.

Income

At the end of every round, each player collects income from the blocks and buildings they own.

Rounds & Events

The game lasts a set number of rounds. City events like storms and festivals will shake things up.

City events arrive in a later phase.

Winning

When the final round ends, the richest mayor — counting cash and property — wins the keys to the city.
